HyperExtend adapts MediaVolumeEntry, MediaPlaybackWatcher and MiSoundAppVolumeHook
ideas/code into hook/feature/volume/AppVolumeEntryHooks.kt, AppVolumePolicy.kt,
MediaPlayback.kt, MiSoundCardHooks.kt and AppVolumeCardGeometry.kt.
These files retain the Apache License 2.0; its full text is included in
META-INF/licenses/HyperVolumeANC-Apache-2.0.txt.

Modifications: Kotlin port, HyperExtend settings and hook runtime, per-view
lifecycle, bounded permission-protected launch requests, native MiSound click
handoff, lockscreen checks and non-accumulating margin calculation, native helper
material initialization, controller-scoped floating-ball suppression, right-side
blur card layout, native slider/pager reuse, guarded slide animations and
viewport-safe card sizing.
No ANC, Bluetooth, third-party earphone protocol or navigation code is included.
MiSoundVolumeHooks.kt is a new implementation based on target-host inspection;
the visual adaptations are in the Apache-2.0 files listed above.
The rest of HyperExtend remains under its stated licenses.
